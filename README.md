# GoGo — Move. Deliver. Discover.

**One app for rides, deliveries, food, shopping, local services, stays and more.**

GoGo is an all-in-one mobility and local-services platform designed to connect people with drivers, couriers and local businesses through one simple experience.

> **For drivers and businesses: simple subscription. No commission on each transaction.**

## What you can do with GoGo

- 🚗 **GoGo Ride** — request rides and schedule trips
- 🏍️ **Deliveries** — local delivery services
- 🍽️ **GoGo Eat** — discover restaurants and order food
- 🛍️ **GoGo Shop** — shop from local businesses
- 🔥 **GazGo** — cooking-gas delivery and related services
- 🏨 **GoGo Stay** — hotels, apartments and accommodation
- ✨ **GoGo Services** — discover useful local services
- 🚌 **GoGo Bus** — bus travel and ticketing *(coming soon)*

## Built around local opportunity

GoGo's model is designed differently from traditional commission-heavy marketplaces. Drivers and participating businesses can operate through a subscription model rather than giving the platform a percentage of every eligible transaction.

**Drive — Earn**  
**Deliver — Earn**  
**Cook — Earn**  
**Sell — Earn**  
**Promote — Earn**

### With GoGo, we dey go!

## Platform highlights

GoGo is being built with location-aware services, multiple languages, ride and order references, receipts, in-app communication, driver tools, business dashboards and support for local payment options.

The platform is designed for international expansion while allowing services and payment options to adapt to each market.

## Our approach

**People first · Safer services · More opportunities · Cleaner cities**

GoGo aims to make everyday mobility and local commerce easier while giving independent drivers and businesses a straightforward way to participate in the digital economy.

## Project status

GoGo is under active development and rollout. Features and availability may vary by country and market.
