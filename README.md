# 🟠 GoGo

## Move. Deliver. Discover.

**One app for rides, deliveries, food, shopping, local services, accommodation and more.**

GoGo is an all-in-one mobility and local-services platform connecting customers with independent drivers, couriers, restaurants, shops, accommodation providers and other local businesses through one simple experience.

> ### 0% commission on eligible rides and orders
> **Drivers and participating businesses use a simple subscription model instead of giving GoGo a percentage of each eligible transaction.**

**Drive — Earn · Deliver — Earn · Cook — Earn · Sell — Earn · Promote — Earn**

### With GoGo, we dey go!

---

## 🌍 What is GoGo?

People often need several different apps to move around a city, order food, arrange a delivery, shop locally, find services or book accommodation. GoGo brings these everyday needs together in a single platform.

The vision is simple: **one local digital ecosystem for people, mobility and commerce.**

GoGo is designed to work market by market, adapting available services, currencies and payment options to the user's location.

## 🚕 GoGo Ride

GoGo Ride is built around a location-aware ride experience.

Customers can:

- use their current location or enter a pickup point
- search for a destination
- view an estimated route, distance and travel time
- choose from available vehicle categories
- request a ride now or schedule one for later
- access upcoming, completed and cancelled rides
- receive a trip reference and receipt where supported

For drivers, GoGo's proposition is deliberately straightforward:

> **You drive. You earn. You keep it.**

GoGo's subscription approach is intended to give participating independent drivers a predictable platform cost rather than a percentage commission on every eligible fare.

---

## 🏪 Built for local businesses

GoGo is not only a ride platform. It is designed as digital infrastructure through which local businesses can become discoverable and serve nearby customers.

Participating restaurants, shops, accommodation providers and service businesses can have their own presence within the GoGo ecosystem, with tools designed around listings, orders/bookings and earnings visibility.

The goal is to help local operators participate in digital commerce without building a complete marketplace of their own.

---

## 💳 Subscription-first business model

Traditional marketplaces often charge providers a percentage of each transaction. GoGo is designed around a different principle.

### Simple subscription. No per-transaction GoGo commission on eligible provider earnings.

This creates a model where the platform's subscription revenue is separated from the money earned by participating providers.

Payment methods and settlement arrangements can vary by country and provider. GoGo is designed so local payment infrastructure can be adapted to each market.

## 🌐 Local by design. Global in ambition.

GoGo is designed so individual services can be introduced according to the needs and regulations of each market rather than forcing every country into an identical configuration.

The platform can support different combinations of rides, delivery, food, shopping, gas, accommodation, services and future transport products depending on local availability.

The initial rollout is focused on building the GoGo ecosystem and validating the subscription-first model before wider expansion.

## 🛡️ Trust and accountability

The platform is being built with features intended to improve transparency and accountability, including driver/provider information, unique trip and order references, receipts, account history and administrative lookup tools.

GoGo does not claim that technology alone guarantees safety. Verification requirements and service rules may vary according to market, provider type and applicable regulation.

---

## 🧭 Product principles

1. **Keep it simple** — everyday services should be easy to find and use.
2. **Keep providers independent** — GoGo is a platform connecting customers and participating providers.
3. **Keep earnings transparent** — the subscription model should be understandable from the start.
4. **Build locally** — currencies, services and payments should adapt to the market.
5. **Think globally** — the underlying GoGo experience should be capable of expanding across markets.
